Paste these on your Laravel/web service → Variables (use "Add variable reference" for the first line).

1) DATABASE_URL  =  ${{MySQL.MYSQL_URL}}
   (Change "MySQL" to match your MySQL service name if different.)

2) Optional (defaults work on Railway if you skip them):
   APP_DEBUG=true
   LOG_CHANNEL=stderr

3) Generate a public domain: Service → Settings → Networking → Generate Domain.

That is enough: APP_URL and APP_KEY are filled automatically on Railway for this repo.
